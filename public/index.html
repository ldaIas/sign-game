About Zodle

Zodle is a free zodiac sign quiz: guess the star sign from a birthday, then test what else you know about it. A quick star sign game to play daily, at your own pace, or against the clock.

Today's birthday, the same for everyone: name its sun sign, symbol, gender, element and opposite sign. You have 5 misses in total.

3 guesses to name each birthday's sign. Get it right, then name its element in one try for a bonus point.

Name as many signs as you can in 60 seconds. One guess per birthday, one point per right answer.
