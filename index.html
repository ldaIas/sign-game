About Zodle

Zodle is a free zodiac sign quiz: guess the star sign from a birthday, then test what else you know about it. A quick star sign game to play daily, at your own pace, or against the clock.

Today’s challenge: name this birthday’s sun sign, then its symbol, gender, element, opposite sign, modality and ruling planet. Your misses are shared across every question.

3 guesses to name each birthday's sign, with a hint after each miss. Birthdays near a sign change are marked tricky and score 2. Get it right, then name its element in one try for a bonus point.

Name as many signs as you can in 60 seconds. One guess per birthday, one point per right answer. On a keyboard, type the first letters of a sign to answer.

Practice games: match each sign to its symbol, or sort all twelve signs by gender, modality or element.

          Privacy

          Privacy

          Zodle doesn’t use cookies or analytics and doesn’t collect personal data. Your scores, daily progress, streaks and game stats, whether you’ve hidden the About card or install tip, and which More game you last played, are saved only in your own browser’s local storage; clearing site data removes them. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request logs to serve and protect the site.

          Accessibility

          Accessibility

          We aim to make Zodle playable with a keyboard (Tab, Enter and Space; in Timed mode you can also type a sign’s first letters) and with a screen reader, with readable colour contrast and less motion when your device asks for it. If something gets in your way, please let us know with the feedback link.
