About Zodle

Zodle is a free zodiac sign quiz: guess the star sign from a birthday, then test what else you know about it. A quick star sign game to play daily, at your own pace, or against the clock.

Today’s challenge: name the sun sign, symbol, gender, element and opposite sign of this birthday. You have 5 misses in total.

3 guesses to name each birthday's sign. Get it right, then name its element in one try for a bonus point.

Name as many signs as you can in 60 seconds. One guess per birthday, one point per right answer.

    Privacy

    Privacy

    Zodle doesn’t use cookies or analytics and doesn’t collect personal data. Your scores, daily progress and streaks, and whether you’ve hidden the About card or install tip, are saved only in your own browser’s local storage; clearing site data removes them. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request logs to serve and protect the site.

    Accessibility

    We aim to make Zodle playable with a keyboard (Tab, Enter and Space) and with a screen reader, with readable colour contrast and less motion when your device asks for it. If something gets in your way, please let us know with the feedback link.
